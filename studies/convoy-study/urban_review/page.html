Which scenarios should we study next?

Keep these six convoy situations as the core learning set. No machine-learning training is needed to inspect them. The Logistics prototype separately retains its five original disruptions.

Next controlled experiments: compare matched crewed/autonomous variants on the same urban route; compare finite halt releases under natural engagement; and study leader recovery with versus without console recovery under identical combat conditions. Use a declared design and multiple seeds before selecting a representative replay.

These additional matched experiments are proposals, not options already simulated here. Timed outages, breakdown/recovery and relocation in the logistics prototype are different mechanisms from fixed C0–C5 conditions, destroyed vehicles and the fixed destination in the convoy model.

      Part 6 · Evidence and limits
      What has been checked — and what remains open?
      Software evidence
      The preceding settings study reported 84 focused tests passing, cargo conservation across 90 runs and retention of all 56 replay options. That is historical verification of that code state; this page does not execute pytest.
      Forced-loss diagnostics verify mechanisms. Natural-engagement missions illustrate the combined behavior. Multi-seed studies estimate outcomes only within their tested settings.
      Model limits
      No adaptive in-mission rerouting, integrated wreck blocking, ambush fire-control template or Red halt-cost mechanism is connected here. Herringbone and console transfer are experimental adapters. Human workload, weapon effectiveness and geometry are not field-calibrated. Vehicle collision avoidance is not guaranteed.
      Issue #19 remains incomplete. Missing comparators, registered values and ownership assumptions must remain visible rather than being presented as solved.
      Source files and provenance
      Embedded data are a snapshot. The page is a playback and explanation tool; it does not recompute Python vehicle behavior. No network service is required for the recordings or equations. Google Fonts is optional; local font fallbacks apply offline.
