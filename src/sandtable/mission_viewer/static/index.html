Advanced settings · crews, clocks, consoles and assumptions
Gap convention

Time gap and minimum gap set centre-reference stations. No bumper clearance is silently added. The initial separation is max(minimum gap, commanded pace × time gap); actual following uses tracked reference speed. Near the goal the formation closes up.

Declared capabilities and limits
  • Fixed initial route, static Red sites and a raster building LOS model.
  • Herringbone and optional console transfer are explicitly experimental V3 study adapters.
  • Steering/arrival and building guards are not realistic truck dynamics, collision avoidance or dynamic rerouting.
  • Weapon parameters and edge exposure priors are assumptions, not calibrated war outcomes.
  • With fixed console ownership, promote/absorb does not restore a destroyed console.

Initial route

Cost = length / trafficability × (1 + 10 × weight × exposure). Weight 0 ignores exposure. A longer route can cost less when exposure is lower. Scores use known Red positions/ranges and building screening; they are not casualty probabilities. Road crossings are connected during validation. Manual paths use adjacent named nodes. No runtime replanning or wreck bypass.
